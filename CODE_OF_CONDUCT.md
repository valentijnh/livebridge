# Code of conduct

LiveBridge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient with people of every background and skill level, whether
they're producers new to code or developers new to music. Critique ideas, not people.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@valentijnh](https://github.com/valentijnh),
privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block
people who break these rules.
