# Prompts to copy

Things you can say to Claude with LiveBridge connected. They are ordinary sentences: mention
tracks, clips and devices by the names you see in Live, and say "this track" or "the selected
clip" for whatever is selected.

## Your set

- What's in my set? Give me tempo, key, tracks, devices and which scenes have clips.
- What key is the clip on the Keys track in, and which chords does it play?
- Which tracks have no instrument yet?
- Show me everything that's muted or soloed.
- List the plug-ins I use and the tracks they're on.
- What's on the Drum Rack pads of the Drums track?
- How long is the arrangement, and where are the locators?
- Is Live running, and which version and edition?

## Beats and drums

- Make an 8-bar house beat at 124 BPM with the 909 kit.
- Give me a boom-bap beat at 90 BPM: kick on 1 and the "and" of 2, snare on 2 and 4, swung hats.
- Write a trap pattern at 140 with 1/32 hat rolls in the last bar.
- Drum & bass at 174: a two-step kick and snare with ghost notes.
- Add ghost snares between the backbeats, velocity around 40.
- Humanize the hi-hats a little: timing and velocity, keep the kick tight.
- Swing the hats by about 20 %.
- Double the hats to 16ths in the second half of the loop.
- Make a 4-bar snare roll that speeds up into the drop.
- Put a crash on bar 1 of every section.
- Take the kick out of the last bar before the drop.
- Load a different kit but keep my pattern.
- Put this sample on pad C1 of my Drum Rack: ~/Samples/kick.wav
- Turn the snare pad into its own track so I can process it separately.

## Bass

- Write an off-beat house bassline that follows the chords on the Pad track.
- A rolling 16th-note bass in A minor, root and octave, for a techno track.
- Make the bass follow the kick pattern, one octave below the chords' roots.
- Load a sub bass on a new track and write a simple two-note line.
- Transpose the bassline down an octave.
- Make the bass notes shorter and punchier.
- Side-chain the bass to the kick.
- Check whether the bass clashes with the chords.

## Chords and harmony

- Write Am7 – Fmaj7 – C – G on a warm pad, two bars each, with smooth voice leading.
- Give me a sad progression in D minor using Roman numerals, i – VI – iv – v.
- Make a neo-soul progression with 9th chords at 85 BPM.
- Voice these chords more open: drop-2 voicings.
- Add a bass note under every chord.
- Strum the piano chords slightly, like a guitarist.
- Change the progression to the relative major.
- What chords am I playing in this clip? Name them with Roman numerals.
- Fit every note of the lead to the song's scale.
- Set Live's scale to F# minor so scale mode highlights the right notes.

## Melody and arps

- Write a simple 4-bar melody over the chords that lands on chord tones on the downbeats.
- Arpeggiate the chords as 16ths going up and down, two octaves.
- Give me a plucky arp in triplets over the progression.
- Make a call-and-response between the lead and the arp.
- Reverse the melody in the second half.
- Invert the melody around C4.
- Add a counter-melody an octave higher with fewer notes.
- Randomize the arp order but keep it repeatable.

## Sound design and instruments

- Load Serum 2 on a new track and make a dark Reese bass.
- In Serum 2, open the filter slowly over 8 bars.
- Put Serum's filter cutoff on macro 1 and the detune on macro 2.
- Start from one of my Serum 2 presets with "pluck" in the name.
- Load Drift and make a short, bright pluck.
- Load Wavetable with a wide supersaw pad.
- Make the pad darker and the attack slower.
- Load an electric piano and add a bit of chorus.
- Randomize the Operator patch, but keep the volume sane.
- What does the Low Pass Filter macro on the Pad do? Turn it up to half.
- Make my third-party VST3 synth controllable so you can tweak its parameters.
- Reset all parameters of this device to their defaults.

## Samples and Splice

- Find a dusty vinyl drum loop on Splice around 90 BPM and show me three options.
- Get me a few one-shot kicks from Splice and put them on a Drum Rack.
- Import the sample I just downloaded from Splice into a new track and warp it.
- What tempo and key is this loop?
- Put ~/Downloads/vocal.wav into a Simpler on a new MIDI track.
- Slice the break in this Simpler into a Drum Rack.
- Pitch the loop down two semitones to match the song key.
- Import these three files into the arrangement, one after another from bar 17.

## Arrangement and song structure

- Turn my scenes into a song: intro 8 bars, verse 16, chorus 16, outro 8.
- Build a classic club structure: intro, build, drop, breakdown, build, drop, outro.
- Add locators for every section.
- Where does my arrangement feel empty? Show me the overview.
- Make the breakdown 16 bars instead of 8.
- Copy bars 17–32 to bar 49.
- Duplicate the chorus and put it at the end.
- Extend the intro loop to fill 16 bars.
- Jump to the drop and loop it.
- Plan the energy curve for a 3-minute melodic techno track, then lay out the sections.

## Automation and movement

- Sweep the filter on the pad up through the build.
- Add a slow sine LFO on the reverb send, one cycle every 4 bars.
- Fade the pad in over the first 8 bars.
- Make the hi-hat volume pulse in 16ths, like a gate.
- Record a tempo ramp from 120 to 128 BPM over the build in the arrangement.
- Copy the filter automation from the verse clip to the chorus clip.
- Show me the automation on this clip.
- Clear all automation on the Bass track clips.

## Mixing and effects

- Set a rough mix: kick at -6 dB, bass -8, pads -14, returns -15.
- Put a low cut at 150 Hz on everything except the kick and the bass.
- Add a compressor to the drums and glue them together.
- Side-chain the pad to the kick for that pumping sound.
- Put a limiter last on the master.
- Pan the hats 20 % left and the shaker 20 % right.
- Send the snare to the reverb return at -12 dB.
- Is anything clipping? Read the meters while the drop plays.
- Compare my mix with ~/Music/reference.wav: loudness and spectral balance.
- Is my low end mono?
- Add a delay return with a dotted-eighth delay.
- Group the drum effects into an Audio Effect Rack.

## Recording and bouncing

- Bounce the first 8 bars of the master to an audio track.
- Resample the Bass track with its effects so I can chop it.
- Arm the Keys track and record 4 bars in the session, starting on the next bar.
- Capture the MIDI I just played.
- Freeze-style: render the Serum track to audio so it costs no CPU.

## Workflow and learning

- Explain what this Drum Rack does, pad by pad.
- Why is the Pad track silent?
- Undo your last three changes.
- Select the Bass track and show its devices.
- Open the clip editor on the drum loop with a 1/16 grid.
- Make a template: drums, bass, chords, lead and two returns, colored and named.
- Walk me through what makes a good build-up, then build one in my set.
- Give me three variations of this loop in new scenes so I can compare.

## Two computers

- Find Live on my network.
- Connect to Live on 192.168.1.20 and remember it.
- Send this sample from my Mac to the Live PC and put it on a new track.

## What doesn't work (limits of Live's API)

- **Saving the set**: press Cmd/Ctrl+S yourself. Claude will remind you.
- **Exporting audio files**: Claude bounces in real time with resampling instead.
- **Hearing**: Claude reads meters and measures bounced audio, but your ears decide.
- **Mapping macros, freezing, grouping tracks, menu commands**: Claude tells you the two clicks
  to make.

More details in the [README limitations](../README.md#limitations).
